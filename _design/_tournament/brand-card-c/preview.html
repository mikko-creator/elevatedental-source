Standing constraints

  • No vector logo master. Raster PNG only at 853 by 268. The lockups here are typographic proposals until a master or a recorded re-trace exists.
  • No real treatment prices. The price atom is a marked placeholder and a swappable pattern, never a quoted term.
  • No documented owner authorisation for the clinical image library, so no before and after imagery is specified on this sheet.
  • Affiliation marks are rights-uncleared, so no logo wall is designed and no slot is reserved for one.
  • Equipment names are nominative text only. No third-party logo, badge or provider-tier claim.
  • Light is the ground. Measured dark ratio 0. Navy appears as punctuation on this sheet and never as a page ground.
