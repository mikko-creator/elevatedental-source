Palette and roles

Green is the register. Navy is the anchor. Gold is the moment of warmth.

Contrast figures are computed against the white ground. The split between bronze and gold is a working rule, not a preference: bronze clears the text floor, gold does not, so gold never carries a word on a light surface.

11.7:1 Anchor#243A52 Heading ink, primary control fill, and the single dark punctuation band if a page ever earns one. Never a hero ground.
6.2:1 Calming register#4F6754 Secondary control, field focus, tag ink. Text safe on white.
Ground Sage wash#EDF1EC The calming tint band the sector blueprint asks for.
4.9:1 Warm accent, text safe#876C33 Eyebrows, field labels, tertiary links.
2.5:1 Decorative only#C69E4C Arc hairline, medallion ring, accent rule. Never body text on light.
Ground Warm neutral#F7F4EE The sheet ground. Warmer than paper, quieter than sage.
Ground Paper#FFFFFF Card and panel ground. Figure against linen.
16.1:1 Body ink#1F2124 Running copy only. Headings take the navy so the two registers stay separable at a glance.

Measured surface character of the live site: light dominance, dark ratio 0, restrained energy. This palette keeps that. Navy at full strength appears on controls and on type, and on at most one band per page. The dark flip of these same tokens is declared in the token sheet so a stray dark section can never inherit a light value.

Signature motif

The circle up arc, and the geometry it sets.

One curve family, used at every section seam and nowhere else. It is drawn in CSS, carries no asset, and is finished with a single gold hairline so the seam reads as intentional rather than merely soft.

Photography treatment

Circle masked, well lit, never faded behind type.

Facility photography is the strongest asset this client holds: real interiors in four real places. It is framed, never overlaid. Type does not sit on a washed out photograph to make itself legible.

Frames in this direction: circle for people and for a single focal place, large radius squircle for wide interiors, consistent radius rectangles for everything else. Clinical before and after imagery is deliberately NOT specified here and is not shown. the required owner authorisation is not on file, so the treatment scoped case gallery may be designed but may not be published.

Controls

One primary, site wide. The appointment is the spine.

The primary label is locked to exact wording and exact capitalisation. Its shape is a navy pill so it carries the anchor colour onto every screen, and the gold appears only as a hairline in the hover state, which is where warmth belongs on a control.
