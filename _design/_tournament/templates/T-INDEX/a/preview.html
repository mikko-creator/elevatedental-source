03
Featured

A place to start, if you do not know which field you need.

A short pinned set sits above the full list. It is not a ranking and it is not the whole roster. It is a small, deliberately chosen group, kept current, so that a first-time visitor who does not yet know whether their case is prosthodontic or periodontic still lands on a real clinician rather than an empty filter box.

Each pinned card carries the same information as every other card on this page: a photograph, the clinician's name, their credentials exactly as published, the field they practice in, and a link to the full profile. Nothing in the pinned set is presented that is not also true of the person in the list below. If none of them matches your case, scroll. The full roster is directly underneath, and the filters are faster than reading.

The card contract, and where each value comes from
Field What it carries State on this template
Photograph The published roster portrait, circle masked at one size. Live for 26 of 31. Five portrait files are not in the asset set.
Name The clinician's name as the practice publishes it. Live. It is also the sort key and the only searchable value.
Credentials The credential string exactly as published, never abbreviated. Held. It is a profile pull, and it is never read off a photograph.
Field The one discipline the clinician practises in. Held. Until it is synced, the field filter stays disabled and says so.
Profile link The whole card is the link, so the tap target is the card. Live. Training, treatments and the field hub sit on the profile.
Owner data, held

The featured selection. Four pinned slots are reserved and the copy above is final, but the selection itself is an owner decision and has not been supplied. It may not be auto-filled from roster order, alphabetical order or recency, and it may not be picked by inference from the profile pages. Each slot needs a name, credentials as published, a field, and one sentence approved by that clinician. Until all four arrive, this band states the reservation and the page opens on the full bench instead, which is why no pinned row is rendered above the roster.
