Cosmetic dentistry

Cosmetic Dentistry Philippines - Veneers and Whitening

Cosmetic dentistry changes the shape, shade and proportion of the teeth you show when you speak.

It is planned work, not a single appointment: your dentist assesses the teeth, the gumline and the way your face frames them, then agrees the design with you before anything is prepared.

At Elevate Dental that plan is drawn against a scan of your actual face, and it is carried out by dentists who work in the same practice as the orthodontists, prosthodontists and periodontists a cosmetic case often needs.

02
The scope

What this field covers

Cosmetic dentistry is the restorative and aesthetic layer of your care. It addresses how a tooth looks, how it sits next to the tooth beside it, and how the whole set reads against your lips and jawline.

The concerns treated in this field

  • Teeth that have darkened with age, coffee, tea or tobacco
  • Chipped, worn or uneven edges
  • Small gaps between the front teeth
  • Teeth that are narrow, short or out of proportion with their neighbors
  • Old composite work that has stained at the margins
  • A gumline that sits high or low against the teeth
  • Front teeth that are structurally sound but do not match

Held in-house

Cosmetic dentistry is one of the dental disciplines Elevate Dental holds in-house, alongside general dentistry, orthodontics, endodontics, periodontics, prosthodontics, oral surgery, pediatric dentistry and dental radiology.

Cosmetic cases are where that matters most. A front tooth that looks wrong is often a bite problem, a gum problem or an alignment problem wearing a cosmetic disguise. When your assessment finds one, the orthodontist or the periodontist who handles it works in the same practice, on the same file, and the plan is revised once rather than restarted.

Who designs your case

The specialists in this field

Every dentist at Elevate Dental is published by name and by discipline, so you can read who is designing your case before you commit to it.

A cosmetic case rarely stays cosmetic.

Veneers on a bite that is closing unevenly will chip. A gumline that is uneven will show through any restoration placed under it.

In a single-dentist practice each of those is a referral out and a plan begun again. Here the prosthodontist, the orthodontist and the periodontist are colleagues on the same case, and your design is revised in one conversation.

06
Measured before it is made

Technology used here

Cosmetic work is measured before it is made. These systems are installed at Elevate Dental and used to plan and check it.

RayFace facial scanner

We use a RayFace 3D facial scanner to plan veneers, crowns and smile design against the proportions of your actual face, rather than against the tooth in isolation.

iTero intraoral scanner

A digital scan of your teeth in place of putty impression trays. The scan is the record your veneers are made to.

OccluSense bite analysis

Digital pressure mapping that shows where your teeth actually meet, checked before new surfaces are fitted so the force lands where it should.

Zoom whitening

Light-activated in-clinic whitening, used on its own or to set the shade the rest of a case is matched to.

Imaging

3D CBCT imaging, available at all four clinics

Mitsukoshi BGC, Shangri-La Plaza, Bonifacio Stopover BGC and Alabang Town Center. It is used when a cosmetic case sits on a tooth whose root or bone support needs assessing first.
