Cosmetic dentistry

Cosmetic Dentistry Philippines - Veneers and Whitening

Cosmetic dentistry changes the shape, shade and proportion of the teeth you show when you speak.

It is planned work, not a single appointment: your dentist assesses the teeth, the gumline and the way your face frames them, then agrees the design with you before anything is prepared.

At Elevate Dental that plan is drawn against a scan of your actual face, and it is carried out by dentists who work in the same practice as the orthodontists, prosthodontists and periodontists a cosmetic case often needs.

Scope

What this field covers

Cosmetic dentistry is the restorative and aesthetic layer of your care.

It addresses how a tooth looks, how it sits next to the tooth beside it, and how the whole set reads against your lips and jawline.

The concerns treated in this field

  • Teeth that have darkened with age, coffee, tea or tobacco
  • Chipped, worn or uneven edges
  • Small gaps between the front teeth
  • Teeth that are narrow, short or out of proportion with their neighbors
  • Old composite work that has stained at the margins
  • A gumline that sits high or low against the teeth
  • Front teeth that are structurally sound but do not match

Cosmetic dentistry is one of the dental disciplines Elevate Dental holds in-house, alongside general dentistry, orthodontics, endodontics, periodontics, prosthodontics, oral surgery, pediatric dentistry and dental radiology. Cosmetic cases are where that matters most. A front tooth that looks wrong is often a bite problem, a gum problem or an alignment problem wearing a cosmetic disguise. When your assessment finds one, the orthodontist or the periodontist who handles it works in the same practice, on the same file, and the plan is revised once rather than restarted.

Which of these applies to you, and which treatment answers it, is settled at the assessment and not on this page. The list above is the scope of the field, not a route from a symptom to a product.

Sequence

How the plan is made

Nothing is prepared until you have agreed to it. That sentence is the whole sequence, and these are the four stages inside it.

  1. The assessment. A cosmetic consultation starts with an assessment of the teeth, the bite and the gumline. What you want changed is read against what is actually there.
  2. The facial scan. A facial scan if your case calls for one, so the design is drawn against the proportions of your actual face rather than against the tooth in isolation.
  3. The proposed design. You see the proposed design and the sequence it will be built in, before any tooth is prepared.
  4. Your agreement. Nothing is prepared until you have agreed to it. If the assessment finds a bite, a gum or an alignment problem underneath, the plan is revised once rather than restarted.

The order is the point. A design agreed before it is built is a design you can still change; a design changed after preparation has begun is a case that has started twice.

The catch

A cosmetic case rarely stays cosmetic

Veneers on a bite that is closing unevenly will chip. A gumline that is uneven will show through any restoration placed under it.

Elsewhere

A referral out, and a plan begun again

In a single-dentist practice each of those findings is a referral to somebody else, and a design that starts over from the beginning.

Here

Colleagues on the same case

The prosthodontist, the orthodontist and the periodontist are colleagues on the same case, and your design is revised in one conversation.

Instruments

What the plan is measured with

Cosmetic work is measured before it is made. These systems are installed at Elevate Dental and used to plan and check it.

01

RayFace facial scanner

A RayFace 3D facial scanner is used to plan veneers, crowns and smile design against the proportions of your actual face, rather than against the tooth in isolation.

02

iTero intraoral scanner

A digital scan of your teeth in place of putty impression trays. The scan is the record your veneers are made to.

03

OccluSense bite analysis

Digital pressure mapping that shows where your teeth actually meet, checked before new surfaces are fitted so the force lands where it should.

04

Zoom whitening

Light-activated in-clinic whitening, used on its own or to set the shade the rest of a case is matched to.

05

3D CBCT imaging

Used when a cosmetic case sits on a tooth whose root or bone support needs assessing first. Installed at Mitsukoshi BGC, Shangri-La Plaza, Bonifacio Stopover BGC and Alabang Town Center.
