The specialists here

What the choice of clinic does not change

Elevate Dental is a group of specialists, and that is true at every address.

Our specialists work by discipline, which means an orthodontic case is planned by an orthodontist, a root canal is performed by an endodontist, and a crown or bridge is designed by a prosthodontist. You are not passed to a generalist who then refers you out to a stranger across the city.

That is the practical value of a specialist bench. A case that touches two disciplines, an implant that needs surgical placement and then a prosthetic restoration, is discussed between the two people who will do the work. The full roster, with each specialist's discipline, is published on the specialists page.

So choose on hours and on distance. The bench does not move.

Before you choose

The five questions this clinic gets

Is this clinic open on Sundays?
Yes. Saturday and Sunday both run 10:30 to 20:00, half an hour earlier than the weekday start. Of the four clinics, only Bonifacio Stopover BGC is closed on Sundays.
What is the earliest appointment I can get here?
Weekdays open at 11:00 and weekends at 10:30. If you need a chair before that, Bonifacio Stopover BGC opens at 09:00 from Monday to Saturday and its direct line is +63 917 829 4551.
Can I still be seen late in the day?
The clinic closes at 20:00 every day of the week. The last appointment of the day is set by treatment length, so a longer procedure needs an earlier slot. Call the direct line and say what the visit is for, and the front desk will tell you which hours are still open.
Is there parking?
Parking is paid and in-building, so you can drive in and come up without leaving the complex. Give yourself a few extra minutes on weekends, when the mall is busiest.
Do the hours change on public holidays?
They can. The holiday schedule has not been supplied for publication, so call the direct line and check before you travel rather than assuming the standard hours hold.
