Who will actually treat me?

The specialists here

Elevate Dental is a group of specialists.

Our specialists work by discipline. You are not passed to a generalist who then refers you out to a stranger across the city.

That is the practical value of a specialist bench. A case that touches two disciplines, an implant that needs surgical placement and then a prosthetic restoration, is discussed between the two people who will do the work.

The full roster, with each specialist's discipline, is published on the specialists page.

What working by discipline means for your case

  • An orthodontic case is planned by an orthodontist. Braces and Invisalign (Clear Aligners) both sit inside orthodontics, and the plan is written by the person who practises it.
  • A root canal is performed by an endodontist. Endodontics is a discipline of its own here rather than a procedure a generalist adds on.
  • A crown or bridge is designed by a prosthodontist. Restorative work that follows surgery is designed by the specialist whose discipline it belongs to.

What imaging is installed at this address?

The technology here

3D CBCT imaging is installed at this clinic.

CBCT is available at all four of our clinics, so the scan your case needs is taken where you are seen.

What the scan shows

A CBCT scan shows bone volume, nerve position and root anatomy in three dimensions.

What it is planned against

Implant placement, impacted extractions and difficult root canals are planned against that three-dimensional record rather than against a flat film.

Why it matters that it is here

A case that needs a three-dimensional scan is scanned in the same building where it is planned and treated, so the record does not have to travel and neither do you.

What is named at group level

Booking at Mitsukoshi BGC

Book at Mitsukoshi BGC, or call the clinic directly on +63 917 159 9284.

How payment works

Elevate Dental does not accept HMO or dental insurance. All treatment is self-pay. HMO coverage cannot be used for dental treatments at our clinics.

Your treatment plan is quoted after clinical assessment, so you know the figure before anything begins. That order is deliberate: the assessment decides what the plan is, and the plan is what the figure is calculated from.
