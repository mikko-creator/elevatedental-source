02
The model

How our pricing works

We charge per treatment. You pay for the treatment you have, at the fee confirmed for your case, and nothing is bundled into a plan you did not ask for.

Three rules, and they hold on every row of the list

  • A starting price, not a quote. Every figure on this page is a starting price. It is the point at which that treatment begins, for a straightforward case, in the standard material.
  • Your fee is set by your case. Your own fee is set once a specialist has examined you and reviewed your imaging.
  • You get it in writing first. It is given to you in writing before any work starts.

Before the first figure

Elevate Dental does not accept HMOs or dental insurance. All treatment is self-pay. HMO coverage cannot be used for dental treatments at our clinics.

That sentence sits here, above the list, rather than under it. It is the first thing that changes the arithmetic, so it is the first thing stated.

04
What moves it

What changes the final fee

A starting price is honest only if you are told what moves it. Four things do.

None of the four is a surcharge and none of them appears after the fact. Each one is a line on the written quote you read before you agree to anything.

  1. Case complexity. A crown on a sound tooth and a crown on a tooth that first needs root canal treatment are not the same piece of work. The starting price covers the treatment itself. Any preparatory treatment your case needs is quoted as its own line.
  2. Material choice. Veneers, crowns and bridges are made in more than one material. Emax porcelain, zirconia and direct composite differ in strength, translucency and stain resistance, and they are priced differently. Your specialist sets out which materials suit your case, and what each one costs, before you choose.
  3. Number of units. Most restorative and cosmetic work is priced per tooth, per unit or per arch. One veneer is one unit. A ten-unit smile design is quoted as ten units plus the design work the case requires. Every row in the tables above says which unit its starting price refers to.
  4. Preparatory work. Some cases need imaging, an extraction, gum treatment or bone preparation before the treatment you came in for can begin. Where that is true, it appears on your written quote before you agree to anything, not afterwards.

Which resolves to one line

Nothing is charged at the desk that was not on the quote you agreed to.

05
Self-pay

We do not accept HMO or dental insurance

Elevate Dental does not accept HMOs or dental insurance. All treatment is self-pay. HMO coverage cannot be used for dental treatments at our clinics.

This is worth stating plainly on the page where you are working out what treatment will cost, because it changes the arithmetic. There is no card to present at the desk and no claim to file afterwards. The figure on your written quote is the figure you settle.

What you get in return is a fee set by your case and by nobody else, quoted in writing before treatment starts, with no third party deciding which treatment or which material you are entitled to.

If you are used to dental care being handled through a provider, raise it at your consultation. Our team will walk you through what your treatment costs and how it is settled, before you commit to anything.

08
The principle

Why we publish starting prices

A starting price is not a quote, and this page has never pretended otherwise.

A patient deciding whether to treat a broken tooth or straighten a bite is deciding about money as much as about dentistry. Being made to book a consultation simply to find out whether a treatment is within reach is a poor way to begin a clinical relationship, and it wastes the patient's morning.

So we publish where every treatment begins. It is enough information to decide whether to take the next step, which is the only decision you are being asked to make right now.

The limit on that

The figure quoted after your examination may be higher than the starting price. If it is, you will be told why, in writing, before anything begins.

Pricing questions

Owner data, held

The free consultation offer. The free first consultation and the written quote that follows it are agent derived rather than owner supplied. Confirm the offer and its scope before launch.

Owner data, held

Whether prices differ by clinic. The intended answer is that they do not, and that the same starting price applies at Mitsukoshi BGC, Shangri-La Plaza, Bonifacio Stopover BGC and Alabang Town Center. Do not publish that answer until the owner has confirmed it holds at all four sites.

  1. Is the consultation really free?

    Yes. The first consultation and the written quote that follows it are provided at no charge. The offer itself is still waiting on owner confirmation, which is recorded beside this list.

  2. Do prices differ by clinic?

    The intended answer is that they do not: the same starting price applies at every one of the four clinics. That answer is held pending owner confirmation and is not published until it has been confirmed for all four sites.

  3. What if I need more than one treatment?

    Your written quote is itemized by treatment, with the sequence set out, so you can see what has to happen first and what can wait. Many cases are staged over several months for clinical reasons rather than financial ones, and your specialist will tell you which parts of your case are urgent and which are not.

  4. Can I get a written quote?

    Yes, and you should not accept a case without one. Every consultation ends with a written quote listing each treatment, the fee for each, and the order they happen in. Take it away and think about it.
