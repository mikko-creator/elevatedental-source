Clear space

Clear space equals the cap height of the wordmark on all four sides. Nothing crosses it: no rule, no photograph edge, no button. This is a decision taken here, because none is documented in the existing brand record.

ElevateDental

Reproduction ceiling

The supplied master is raster only: a single image at 853 by 268 pixels, plus two smaller derivatives. That is the hard ceiling for any use, and there is no clean source for print or large format.

Working floor for the four-tile set above: the index ring sheds its minor graduations below 40 pixels so the mark stays readable at favicon scale.

Recorded, not solved

The wordmark shown on this sheet is a typeset stand-in. No vector master exists in the supplied asset set, and the build may re-trace the raster but may not invent a new mark. Every lockup here specifies ground, placement, clear space and minimum size; the artwork itself is pending a supplied vector master or a recorded re-trace.

What changed from the record

Nothing was added and nothing was retired. The six colors already in use were given roles, and two derivations were added: a deeper navy for the single punctuation band, and a darker bronze for small text that has to clear 4.5:1.

The gold constraint

Gold reaches only 2.5:1 on white, so it is never text on a light ground. It appears as a mark, a hairline, or a foreground on the navy, where it clears 4.7:1.

Ratio of use

Roughly 78 percent light ground, 15 percent navy structure, 4 percent ink, 3 percent bronze. Bronze earns its weight by scarcity: it is the only color that ever marks a measurement.

04
Signature

The index ring

One motif, one job: mark a position exactly. A thin navy ring, eleven minor graduations, and a single bronze index at twelve o'clock. It is a calibration mark, not a logo, and it never carries a value.

The ring, unrolled: the section-opening rule

How it behaves

The ring unrolls into a straight graduated rule, and that rule is the section opener across the site. The bronze index marks the position of the thing being introduced, so the motif does structural work rather than sitting on the page as decoration.

What it may never do

It never carries a value, never becomes a chart, never rotates, never appears more than twice in one viewport, and never renders in gold on a light ground.

Section opener. Graduated rule above every eyebrow. Card marker. A 2 px bronze index on the card's top edge. List bullet. A single 6 px tick, never a dot. Small mark. Index only at 34 px and below.
05
Measure

Radius, rules and the seam

The instrumentation layer. Three radius roles, four rule weights, and one section seam. These are the values every component on the site inherits without re-deciding them.

Radius roles

Control covers buttons, inputs, tags and index chips. Surface covers cards, panels and media frames. Pill is deliberately rationed to two things on the whole site: the availability status marker and its dot. A rounded interface would soften the argument this concept is making.

Rule weights

1px hairline .16
1px bronze index
2px navy structural
1px navy graduated

Hairlines separate. The bronze rule marks. The 2 pixel navy rule opens a section and appears once per block. The graduated rule is the unrolled motif, used only where a range or a sequence is being shown.

Section seam

A shallow arc, filled in the destination ground, with a single bronze hairline riding the curve. Amplitude is capped at 34 pixels so the seam reads as a machined radius. Used at most twice per page; every other boundary is a flat color step.

Action lockup

One primary label site-wide, one secondary beside it, one text link after. The primary carries a short gold rule that extends on hover, which is the only motion any button on this site performs.

Focus
A 2px bronze outline at 3px offset on every control. Never removed, never traded for a shadow.

Label discipline: the primary reads exactly "Book an Appointment" everywhere it appears, in that capitalisation. Secondaries are chosen per context from a fixed short list, and a page carries at most one.
