After you send

What happens next

The clinic you selected picks up the request, confirms a time with you directly, and tells you what to bring.

  1. The request lands at the clinic. The branch you chose on the form picks it up. There is no central queue it waits in first.
  2. The time is confirmed with you directly. The clinic agrees a slot with you and tells you what to bring to it.
  3. You arrive 15 minutes early. That is enough time to complete any necessary paperwork, or you can complete the forms in advance from the New Patient Guide.
  4. The first appointment is a comprehensive exam. Digital X-rays if they are needed, and a discussion of your options.
  5. You leave knowing the plan. What is happening, what can be done, and in what order.
Before you book

One thing to know before you book: Elevate Dental does not accept HMO or dental insurance. All treatment is self-pay.
