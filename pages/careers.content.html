Why the bench matters here

Most groups grow by opening more of the same clinic. This one widened what one clinic can finish.

That changes the job.

A case that crosses disciplines is planned by the specialists who carry out each stage of it, so you work alongside the orthodontist, the surgeon or the prosthodontist rather than sending a referral letter and losing sight of the outcome.

If you want to see how your work resolves rather than where it went, that is the practical difference, and it is the reason the roles below are posted against a discipline rather than against a headcount plan.
