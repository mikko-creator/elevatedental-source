Your first visit

Please arrive 15 minutes early to complete any necessary paperwork. Our team will guide you through a comprehensive exam, digital X-rays if they are needed, and a discussion of your oral health.

Allow time. A first appointment is not a fifteen-minute check.

  1. Arrive 15 minutes early. Paperwork is completed at the desk, or in advance using the forms in the next section.
  2. Comprehensive exam. A specialist examines your teeth, your gums and your bite.
  3. Imaging, if it is needed. Digital X-rays are taken in clinic. 3D CBCT imaging is installed at all four clinics, so a case that needs a three-dimensional view does not go anywhere else for it.
  4. A discussion of your options. What is happening, what can be done about it, in what order, and what each route involves.

Money
The fact most likely to change your mind

We do not accept HMO or dental insurance

Elevate Dental does not accept HMOs or dental insurance, and HMO coverage cannot be used for dental treatments at our clinics.

All treatment is self-pay. This sits here, in the middle of the page, rather than at the bottom of an accordion. It is the single fact most likely to change your decision, and you are entitled to it before you book rather than at the desk.

What is available instead

  • A starting price for every treatment. The price list publishes a starting figure per treatment so you can size a plan before you walk in.
  • Instalment arrangements through partner banks, where a partnership is currently in force. The next section sets out what that does and does not mean.
  • A consultation first. The cost of your plan is set out before treatment begins, not after it finishes.

Questions we are asked most

Is parking available at your clinics?

Yes. Paid parking is available inside the building at all four: Mitsukoshi BGC, Shangri-La Plaza, Bonifacio Stopover BGC and Alabang Town Center.

Do you accept HMOs or dental insurance?

No. We do not accept HMO or dental insurance, and coverage of that kind cannot be applied to dental treatment at any of our clinics. Treatment is self-pay, and the practice states that in its own words in the payment section above.

What are the credentials of the dentists here?

Our dentists are board-certified and licensed, with local and international postgraduate training, and they continue postgraduate education and hands-on workshops. Each one is published individually with name, credentials and discipline on the Specialists page. Read the profile of whoever you are booking with before you book.

What payment methods do you take?

Cash, bank cheque, bank transfer, debit and credit cards, and QR payments. Arrangements for paying over time are covered in the section above, and depend on which bank partnerships are in force.

What diagnostic and imaging services are available in clinic?

Panoramic, cephalometric and periapical dental X-rays. 3D CBCT imaging, installed at each of the four. Digital scanning with the iTero intraoral scanner and the RayFace facial scanner for impressions and smile planning. Imaging is done where you are treated, so a diagnosis does not wait on an appointment somewhere else.

Do you run promotions?

Offers run periodically. Whatever is running now is listed on the Promos page with the date it ends. An offer past its end date is archived, not displayed.

Is emergency or after-hours care available?

This one is not answered yet, and the reason is recorded beside this list rather than hidden. See the held note on emergency care.

What do you not offer?

Sedation dentistry is not evidenced in our current published material and is not claimed here. Treatment warranty terms are not published. If a plan you are considering depends on either, ask the clinic directly before you commit to it.

Whether this practice offers an emergency service is not stated here, in either direction. If you need urgent care, call the clinic and ask rather than infer an answer from this page.

A complimentary first consultation has appeared repeatedly inside dated promotions and never as a standing offer.

Bring the completed forms, arrive 15 minutes early, and allow time for a full exam and a proper discussion of your options. One direct line is published per clinic, on the Contact page and at the foot of every page.
