What this field covers

What is watched, and when

Bring your child in when the first teeth appear rather than when something hurts. Pediatric dentistry is the preventive layer of your child's care, delivered at a pace a child can follow. Each step is explained before it is done, and the appointment is unhurried by design.

  • Decay. In baby teeth, which spreads faster than in adult teeth.
  • Deep grooves. In newly erupted molars, sealed before decay reaches them.
  • Enamel. That needs strengthening in a child at higher risk of cavities.
  • Timing. Baby teeth late to fall out, or adult teeth late to arrive.
  • Crowding. Visible in the mixed dentition, when both baby and adult teeth are present.
  • Habits. Thumb sucking and the rest of the habits that affect how the jaws develop.
  • Injury. Chipped or knocked teeth after a fall.
  • The next ten years. Brushing technique, diet, and the routine that decides them.
The referral that does not happen

Half of what is found in a child's mouth belongs to another field.

Pediatric dentistry is one of the dental disciplines Elevate Dental holds in-house, alongside general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, endodontics, periodontics, prosthodontics, oral surgery and dental radiology. For a child that has a practical meaning. When a check-up shows crowding that will need orthodontics, or a tooth that has to come out to let another through, the specialist who handles it is in the same practice. Your child is not sent to an unfamiliar clinic to start the conversation over, and you are not managing two sets of appointments in two places.

Treatments in this field

The two routes, side by side

Two treatments sit inside this field. They are not two versions of one thing: they protect different surfaces, they are applied differently, and they are repeated on different schedules.

Route 01 Grooves

Dental Sealants

A thin protective coating bonded into the deep grooves on the chewing surfaces of your child's molars, where a toothbrush cannot reach and decay usually begins. Applied without drilling, in a single short appointment, and checked at each recall.

What a matrix cannot decide

Every row above is true of the treatment in general. None of it is true of your child until someone has looked. Two children can arrive the same age with the same brushing routine and leave with different plans, because how deep the grooves are, how much sugar the day contains and how the last recall went are what actually choose.

Sealants are quoted per tooth and fluoride per application in most practices, so the number of teeth being treated changes any total. Where a figure lands it will be a starting figure, and the final fee is confirmed at consultation.

The specialists in this field

Who else is in the room

Every dentist on this bench is published by name and by field, so you can read who will be treating your child before the appointment rather than after it.

A child's mouth changes for a decade, and the changes cross fields. Crowding becomes an orthodontic case. A tooth lost early becomes a spacing problem. A knocked front tooth becomes a restorative one.

If the crowding is the finding

Orthodontics

Crowding seen in the mixed dentition is an orthodontic question, and the point of seeing it early is that the answer can wait for the right moment instead of being forced later.

Orthodontics
If a tooth has to come out

Oral Surgery

A baby tooth that will not shed can hold up the adult tooth behind it. Removing one to let another through is a surgical decision, taken on the imaging rather than on the calendar.

Oral Surgery
If a tooth is chipped or decayed

General Dentistry

A knocked front tooth after a fall, or decay that has gone past the point prevention can reach, is restorative work. It is the first thing an examination looks for and the first thing to settle.

Technology used here

Seeing what is needed, and no more

With children the aim is to see what is needed and no more. These systems are installed here and used with that in mind.

01

Digital radiography

Low-dose digital images, taken only where an examination cannot answer the question, and viewable on screen in the same appointment so you can see what your dentist is describing.

02

3D CBCT imaging

Reserved for cases where a flat image is not enough, such as an unerupted or impacted adult tooth.

03

iTero intraoral scanner

A digital scan in place of putty impression trays, which is easier for a child to sit through and is the record an early orthodontic assessment is built from.

04

AirFlow (guided biofilm therapy)

Cleaning with warm water, air and a fine powder rather than hand scraping, used where your dentist judges it suitable for your child.

The first appointment

Start before there is anything to fix

A first appointment here is a look, a count and a conversation.

  1. The look. Your child's teeth are examined and the risk of decay is assessed, at a pace that lets a child watch what is happening.
  2. The count. What has erupted, what is still to come, and whether anything is running early or late for the age.
  3. What to watch for. You leave knowing what to watch at home and when to come back, which is the whole point of a preventive appointment.
  4. If something needs treating. It is explained to both of you before it is done, never after.
