02
The featured set

The pinned cases are empty by design, and here is exactly why.

A small pinned set sits above the full list. These are chosen for what they demonstrate, not for how recent they are.

Cases where the plan was the interesting part, where more than one discipline was involved, or where the starting position was the kind most people assume cannot be treated. Each featured case carries the treatment performed, the disciplines involved and the number of visits it took, so you can read it as a piece of clinical work rather than as a picture.

None of that is on this page yet, and none of it is being approximated. Publishing a clinical photograph of a person, or the words they wrote about their own treatment, is not a design decision. It is a permission, and the practice does not have it in writing.

Per case, what unlocks a slot

Eight fields, and the first one is the only one that is not editorial.

  1. The signed release. Naming this website as a place of publication. Nothing else on this list matters without it.
  2. A case reference, so the record can be traced back at the clinic.
  3. The treatment performed, named as the treatment page names it.
  4. The disciplines involved, which is the part that shows how the case was actually planned.
  5. The number of visits and the clinic it was carried out at.
  6. Before and after images, same angle, same lighting, because a change of lighting is not a change of teeth.
  7. One line on what the case required, and a link to the treatment page it belongs to.

03
The five segments

The gallery is split by treatment, not poured into one carousel.

A patient researching veneers has no use for scrolling through implant cases to find them. Each segment below states what it holds, and what the case is planned on before anything is fitted.

Segment 01 Veneers

Porcelain, zirconia and direct composite, shown by material.

Material is what drives both the look and the fee, so the segment is split by it rather than by how recent the case is. Veneer cases are planned on a RayFace facial scanner, so the proportions are set against the whole face rather than against the tooth in isolation.

Porcelain veneers
Segment 02 Invisalign (Clear Aligners)

Grouped by what was being corrected.

Crowding, spacing, or a bite issue. Alignment cases are planned on an iTero intraoral scanner, which produces the digital model the aligner sequence is built from. Course lengths differ case by case, and the card states the actual length rather than an average.

Invisalign
Segment 03 Implants

Single tooth, multiple teeth and full arch.

Every implant case is planned on 3D CBCT imaging, which is available at all four clinics, so the bone is assessed in three dimensions before anything is placed. The card states how many implants were placed and over how long.

Dental implants
Segment 04 Whitening

Photographed against a shade guide, not against better lighting.

Zoom whitening is light activated and carried out in the clinic in a single session. Whitening cases are shown against a shade guide rather than against a change in lighting, which is the honest way to photograph them and the reason this segment is the slowest to fill.

Teeth whitening
Segment 05 Full mouth rehabilitation

The cases that need more than one discipline.

Prosthodontics for the restorations, periodontics for the foundation, oral surgery where teeth have to come out first. Bite is checked with OccluSense bite analysis before the final work is fitted. These cases run over months, and the card says how many.

All treatments
