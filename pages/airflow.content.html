Stage 01, plain English

What Airflow cleaning is

Plaque is not a coating you can scrub off in one pass. It is a biofilm: a living, organized layer of bacteria that binds itself to enamel and to the root surface just under the gum line, and rebuilds within hours of being disturbed.

Left alone it hardens into calculus, and calculus is what inflames gums. This is the instrument built for that specific problem. A handpiece delivers warm water, compressed air and a fine powder in one jet. The powder is soft enough to be used on enamel, on exposed root surfaces, on implant necks and around orthodontic brackets, and the jet reaches the pits, fissures and interdental spaces a polishing cup cannot enter.

Your appointment starts with a disclosing solution that stains the film a visible colour. You can see exactly where it sits, and so can your dentist. That is the guided part of guided biofilm therapy: nothing is treated by guesswork, and at the end of the session you are shown the same surfaces again. Marks from coffee, tea, red wine, soy and tobacco come away with it.

The honest limit

Where hardened calculus is present, the powder jet alone will not remove it. Ultrasonic scaling is added in the same visit for those deposits. The two run in the same visit, not as alternatives, and nobody at this practice will tell you the jet replaces the scaler.

Whitening, not cleaning

This does not whiten teeth. It removes what is sitting on top of the enamel and returns the tooth to its own natural shade. If you want to go lighter than that shade, the treatment is teeth whitening and it is booked separately.

Stage 03, chart to recall

How the appointment runs

Six steps, in this order, with the time each one takes at the chair.

Nothing is removed until the deposit has been made visible to both of you, and nothing is called finished until the same surfaces have been checked again with the same agent.

Step 01 10 to 15 min

Consultation and assessment

Your dentist reviews your medical history, examines the soft tissue, and charts gum condition and pocket depths. This is where a routine clean is separated from periodontal treatment.

Step 02 About 5 min

Disclosing

A disclosing solution is applied so the deposit becomes visible. You are shown it before anything is removed.

Step 03 10 to 20 min

Powder jet removal

The handpiece works surface by surface, including the interdental spaces and the gum margin. Powder, water temperature and pressure are set to your sensitivity.

Step 04 5 to 15 min

Ultrasonic scaling where required

Hardened deposit above and just below the gum margin is removed with an ultrasonic tip.

Step 05 About 5 min

Finish and check

Surfaces are rechecked with the disclosing agent, and fluoride is applied where it is indicated.

Step 06 About 5 min

Home care and recall

You leave with a specific routine for the areas that were holding deposit, and a return date set to your case rather than to a default.

Your calendar

Most appointments run 45 to 60 minutes end to end. A first visit, or a visit after a long gap, runs longer.

Stage 07, what changes

What the appointment leaves behind

We do not publish a result for this treatment, because no patient has signed a release for one.

A completed session leaves the enamel clean to the touch, the gum margins free of the film that was inflaming them, and the marking lifted off the surface.

Gum tenderness and bleeding on brushing usually settle within a few days once the load is gone and your home routine keeps it down.

What this does not do is change the colour of your teeth beyond returning them to their own shade. That distinction is the whole of turning 01 at stage 02, and it is the single most common reason a patient books the wrong appointment.

General Dentistry Treatment Prices

Before you book

How is this different from a regular clean?

Oral prophylaxis is the routine scale and polish. This is a specific instrument aimed at the living film and at surface marking, guided by a disclosing agent so treatment follows what is actually there. In many appointments both are used together.

How often should I have it?

Most patients are recalled every six months. If you smoke, wear fixed braces, have implants, or have a history of gum disease, your dentist may set a shorter interval. That decision is made from your chart at the end of the visit.

Will it whiten my teeth?

No. It lifts what is sitting on the enamel and returns the tooth to its natural shade. Going lighter than that is teeth whitening, which is a different treatment with a different appointment and a different fee.

Do I need imaging first?

Not for a routine clean. If your gum assessment shows pocketing or suspected bone loss, imaging is taken so the periodontal picture is measured rather than assumed.

In the chair and afterwards

Does it hurt?

Most patients describe it as a cool spray with light pressure. A bared root surface can be tender. Tell your dentist as it happens and the powder, the water temperature and the pressure are adjusted for you.

Is it safe with braces, implants, crowns or veneers?

Yes, and it is often the preferred instrument around them, because the jet reaches into places a rubber cup cannot and does not scratch restorative surfaces the way an abrasive paste can.

Can I eat and drink straight after?

Yes. For the first few hours, avoid coffee, tea, red wine, dark sauces and tobacco: the enamel surface is at its most receptive immediately after a clean.

Which clinic can I book?

We have four clinics in Metro Manila: Mitsukoshi BGC, Shangri-La Plaza, Bonifacio Stopover BGC and Alabang Town Center. Which of them schedule this treatment, and on which days, is being confirmed with the practice, so book through the appointment control and you will be placed at the branch that runs your case.

Stage 09, the end of the route

Aftercare, and what a first visit gives you

The first few hours matter more than the rest of the month, and the daily routine matters more than both.

  • Brush twice daily with a soft brush and fluoride toothpaste.
  • Clean between the teeth every day, with floss or interdental brushes, and give particular attention to the areas your dentist showed you.
  • Avoid staining foods, drinks and tobacco for the first few hours.
  • If a tooth stays sensitive after a week, or a gum area bleeds persistently, call us rather than waiting for your recall.

A first visit produces three things: a charted assessment of your gums, a clean carried out against what the disclosing agent actually showed, and a fee in writing. None of them commits you to a course of treatment.
