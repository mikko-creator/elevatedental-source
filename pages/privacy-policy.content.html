Privacy Policy

Interim notice, published 24 August 2026. No expiry.

This is an interim notice, not a finished privacy policy. It is deliberately short, it states only what can be checked, and it makes no commitment on the practice's behalf. The written policy will replace it in full.

This page exists so that the line beside our appointment form leads somewhere real.

The written privacy policy for Elevate Dental is being prepared. Until it is finished and signed off, we would rather publish an honest short notice than a long document that looks settled and is not. A policy is a commitment about patient records, and an approximation of one is not a commitment.

Nothing written here reduces any right you already hold in law.

What this website asks you for

This part is not pending, because it is observable. The appointment form is the only place on this site that asks you for anything, and these are its fields.

  • Full name. So the appointment can be put in a name at the desk.
  • Contact number. The clinics confirm appointments by phone.
  • Email address. An alternative route back to you.
  • Preferred clinic. Which of the four branches you want to be seen at.
  • Treatment you are interested in. So the right clinician is the one who calls you back.
  • Preferred date. Checked against the chair diary.
  • Message. Optional, and yours to leave blank.

There is no other form on this site. No field asks for clinical history, and no field asks for payment details.

What is not settled yet

A finished policy answers questions about how this practice runs: what is collected and why, how long a record is held, who inside the practice may open it, whether anything is shared outside it and with whom, and how you ask for a copy, a correction or a deletion.

Every one of those answers belongs to the clinic and its adviser. Writing plausible ones here would be guessing on their behalf, in a document a patient is entitled to rely on.

How to ask in the meantime

Ask a person. If you want to know what the practice holds about you, or you would rather not send details through a form at all, call the branch you attend or email the practice and put the question directly.
