How the group came to be

There are four Elevate Dental clinics today, all in Metro Manila.

Mitsukoshi BGC is the flagship, on the second floor at 8th Avenue corner 36th Street in Grand Central Park, Taguig. Shangri-La Plaza sits on the fourth floor of the East Wing at EDSA corner Shaw Boulevard in Ortigas. Bonifacio Stopover BGC is a short walk from the flagship. Alabang Town Center serves the south.

Each opening added disciplines rather than duplicating them. That is the pattern worth noticing: the group grew by widening what could be finished in-house, not by repeating a general practice in four postcodes.

The argument

Why we built it this way

Assembling this many disciplines inside one practice is expensive. It is also deliberate.

Most complex dental work in Metro Manila is split across practices.

  1. A general dentist starts it.
  2. An orthodontist takes a stage.
  3. A surgeon takes another.
  4. A prosthodontist finishes.

Every handoff is a fresh set of records, a fresh consultation, and a fresh judgment about work somebody else planned. The patient carries the continuity, which is the one thing a patient is least equipped to carry.

Our specialists work under one roof.

A case that needs surgery, then orthodontics, then a final restoration is planned once, by the people who will each do a part of it.

The imaging travels with the case instead of being repeated. The person fitting the final restoration was in the room when the sequence was set.

Stated plainly

That is a structural argument, not a slogan. It is also the reason the numbers on this page are stated exactly rather than described, and the reason every band below this one hands you the thing being counted instead of the count.

The operating model

The bench, and how it works

The bench is the operating model here, not a staff page.

Planned once

A case that crosses disciplines is planned by the specialists who will carry out each stage of it, so the sequence is agreed before the first appointment rather than negotiated between practices halfway through.

Published individually

Every specialist is published individually, with name, credentials and discipline. Nobody on the bench is presented to you only as part of a group.

Read before you book

Read the profile of whoever you are booking with before you book. That is a normal thing to do here, and the roster is published in a form that makes it possible.

One practice, one record

Where a case needs a second discipline, the second discipline is in the same practice, so the imaging and the plan carry forward instead of restarting.

Where to go next

The roster page lists every specialist with the discipline each one practices, so the sensible order is to read the discipline you need, then the person, then book.
