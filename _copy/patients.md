## §2 - Your first visit

**H2:** Your first visit

**Body:** Please arrive 15 minutes early to complete any necessary paperwork. Our team will guide you through a comprehensive exam, digital X-rays if they are needed, and a discussion of your oral health. Allow time. A first appointment is not a fifteen-minute check.

**The sequence, in order:**

1. **Arrive 15 minutes early.** Paperwork is completed at the desk, or in advance using the forms in the next section.
2. **Comprehensive exam.** A specialist examines your teeth, your gums and your bite.
3. **Imaging, if it is needed.** Digital X-rays are taken in clinic. 3D CBCT imaging is installed at all four clinics, so a case that needs a three-dimensional view does not go anywhere else for it.
4. **A discussion of your options.** What is happening, what can be done about it, in what order, and what each route involves.

**Before the day:** If you have an urgent question before your scheduled appointment, contact the Front Desk Officer at your chosen clinic directly. The four direct lines are on the Contact page and in the footer of every page.

## §7 - Questions we are asked most

**H2:** Questions we are asked most

**Q: Is parking available at your clinics?**
Yes. Paid parking is available inside the building at all four: Mitsukoshi BGC, Shangri-La Plaza, Bonifacio Stopover BGC and Alabang Town Center.

**Q: Do you accept HMOs or dental insurance?**
No. "Elevate Dental does not accept HMOs or dental insurance, and HMO coverage cannot be used for dental treatments at our clinics." Treatment is self-pay.

**Q: What are the credentials of the dentists here?**
Our dentists are board-certified and licensed, with local and international postgraduate training, and they continue postgraduate education and hands-on workshops. Each one is published individually with name, credentials and discipline on the Specialists page. Read the profile of whoever you are booking with before you book.

**Q: What payment methods do you take?**
Cash, bank cheque, bank transfer, debit and credit cards, and QR payments. Instalment arrangements are covered in the section above, and depend on which bank partnerships are in force.

**Q: What diagnostic and imaging services are available in clinic?**
Panoramic, cephalometric and periapical dental X-rays. 3D CBCT imaging, installed at all four clinics. Digital scanning with the iTero intraoral scanner and the RayFace facial scanner for impressions and smile planning. Imaging is done where you are treated, so a diagnosis does not wait on an appointment somewhere else.

**Q: Do you run promotions?**
Offers run periodically. Whatever is running now is listed on the Promos page with the date it ends. An offer past its end date is archived, not displayed.

**Q: Is emergency or after-hours care available?**
`[OWNER-DATA - NOT ESTABLISHED. Neither an emergency service nor the absence of one is evidenced anywhere on the current site, and this is an open owner decision. No answer is published here until the client states the position. Asserting either way would be an invention, and this is the question a patient in pain asks first.]`

**Q: What do you not offer?**
Sedation dentistry is not evidenced in our current published material and is not claimed here. Treatment warranty terms are not published. If a plan you are considering depends on either, ask the clinic directly before you commit to it.
