## §2 - What this field covers

Oral surgery is the surgical layer of your care. Your surgeon assesses the tooth, the bone supporting it and the structures around it, then plans the procedure and the healing that follows it.

The cases treated in this field:

- Teeth too broken or decayed to restore
- Impacted wisdom teeth, including those lying against the nerve or the neighboring root
- Surgical extraction where a tooth will not come out whole
- Dental implants placed in the jaw to replace a missing tooth
- Bone grafting where the site has lost the volume an implant needs
- Exposure of an impacted tooth so an orthodontist can bring it into position
- Removal of cysts and lesions in the jaw

Oral surgery is one of the dental disciplines Elevate Dental holds in-house, alongside general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, endodontics, periodontics, prosthodontics, pediatric dentistry and dental radiology. Surgery is almost never the whole treatment. An extraction leaves a gap that a prosthodontist fills. An implant needs a crown made for it. An exposed canine is handed to the orthodontist to move. At Elevate Dental those handovers happen between colleagues, on one set of images, so the surgical plan and the restorative plan are made together rather than one after the other by strangers.

Every procedure is carried out with local anesthesia, and your surgeon takes you through what to expect during the appointment and through the healing period before you consent to it.

## §5 - Technology used here

Surgical planning depends on seeing the anatomy before you reach it. These systems are installed at Elevate Dental and used for that.

- **3D CBCT imaging.** Available at all four clinics: Mitsukoshi BGC, Shangri-La Plaza, Bonifacio Stopover BGC and Alabang Town Center. It shows the nerve canal, the sinus floor and the volume of bone at the site, which is what decides whether an implant can be placed directly or the site needs grafting first.
- **Piezotome.** Ultrasonic instrumentation that cuts bone selectively and leaves soft tissue intact, used in extractions and implant site preparation.
- **Digital radiography.** Low-dose digital images taken through treatment and at review, viewable on screen in the same appointment.
