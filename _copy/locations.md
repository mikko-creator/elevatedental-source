## §2 - Choosing a clinic

Three things usually decide it.

**Where you are.** Two of the four clinics sit in Bonifacio Global City, a few minutes apart, and they are not interchangeable: one is inside Mitsukoshi BGC on 8th Avenue, the other is at Bonifacio Stopover Pavilion. Shangri-La Plaza sits on the EDSA and Shaw Boulevard corner in Mandaluyong, which suits Ortigas Center and the routes north. Alabang Town Center covers the south.

**When you can come.** Hours are genuinely different at each site, so check the card below before you travel. Bonifacio Stopover opens earliest, at 09:00, and it is the only clinic closed on Sundays. Mitsukoshi BGC and Alabang Town Center run latest, closing at 20:00 seven days a week. Shangri-La Plaza is open every day of the week but closes at 19:00.

**Which specialist your case needs.** This is the one thing this page cannot yet answer site by site. See section 4.

If your case is straightforward, book the clinic nearest you. If it is complex, call the direct line for the clinic you are considering and describe the case before you book. The team will tell you where and when the right specialist is available.
