## §3 - Why we built it this way

**H2:** Why we built it this way

**Body:** Assembling this many disciplines inside one practice is expensive. It is also deliberate.

Most complex dental work in Metro Manila is split across practices. A general dentist starts it. An orthodontist takes a stage. A surgeon takes another. A prosthodontist finishes. Every handoff is a fresh set of records, a fresh consultation, and a fresh judgment about work somebody else planned. The patient carries the continuity, which is the one thing a patient is least equipped to carry.

Our specialists work under one roof. A case that needs surgery, then orthodontics, then a final restoration is planned once, by the people who will each do a part of it. The imaging travels with the case instead of being repeated. The person fitting the final restoration was in the room when the sequence was set.

That is a structural argument, not a slogan. It is also the reason the numbers on this page are stated exactly rather than described.
