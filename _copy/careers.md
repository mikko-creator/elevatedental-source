## §2 - Featured

**H2:** Why the bench matters here

**Body:** Most dental groups grow by opening more of the same clinic. This one grew by widening what a single clinic can finish. That changes the job.

A case that crosses disciplines is planned by the specialists who carry out each stage of it, so you work alongside the orthodontist, the surgeon or the prosthodontist rather than sending a referral letter and losing sight of the outcome. If you want to see how your work resolves rather than where it went, that is the practical difference.

**What you would be working with:** 3D CBCT imaging, installed at all four clinics. The iTero intraoral scanner. The RayFace facial scanner. OccluSense bite analysis. The Sol Dental Laser. AirFlow. Zoom whitening. Piezotome. Eight named systems, in daily clinical use, at four clinics.

**Featured roles:** A small pinned set above the list, chosen for the roles the group most needs filled rather than by posting date alone.

## §3 - The list

**H2:** Open roles

**Body:** One card per role. Every card carries the clinic and the discipline, because in this group those two facts define the job more than the title does.

**Item template, declared once and repeated per role:**

- **Role title.**
- **Clinic.** Named, not "Metro Manila".
- **Discipline.** The clinical area the role sits in, or "clinic operations" for non-clinical roles.
- **Employment type.** Full time, part time, or locum.
- **Date posted.**
- **What the role does.** Three to five lines on the actual work, the cases, and who the role reports to.
- **What is required.** Licensure, postgraduate training, and years in the discipline where relevant.
- **Action.** Apply Now.
- **Filter data carried per card:** category, tags, role, clinic, date posted.

**Roles currently listed on the existing site:** associate dentist at Alabang Town Center, front desk coordinator at Mitsukoshi BGC, dental assistant at Bonifacio Stopover BGC, dental assistant at Shangri-La Plaza. All four full time.
