## §2. How Our Treatments Are Organized

This site is arranged the way the clinic is arranged: by field first, treatment second.

A field of dentistry is not a marketing category. It is a separate training path with its own specialists, its own instruments and its own way of reading a case. General dentistry keeps a healthy mouth healthy. Endodontics saves a tooth that is already infected. Periodontics treats the gum and bone that hold teeth in place. Prosthodontics replaces what has been lost. Orthodontics moves teeth into position. Oral surgery removes and rebuilds. Pediatric dentistry looks after children on their own terms. Cosmetic dentistry changes how a smile reads. Digital radiography sees what none of the others can see from the outside.

Each field has a hub page. The hub explains the field in plain language, lists every treatment inside it, states what that treatment starts at, and routes you to the specialists who perform it. Twenty treatments sit across those nine hubs, and none of them sends you elsewhere.

That is the point of the structure. Complex work should not mean being referred somewhere else. A case that needs an oral surgeon and then a prosthodontist is handled by one bench of 31 specialists under one roof, working from a single set of records, in the clinic you already chose.

Nine fields. Twenty treatments. Every starting figure below is a representative placeholder pending the owner's confirmed price list, and is marked as such.

**General Dentistry.** Three treatments. From ₱2,500 [REPRESENTATIVE-PLACEHOLDER].

Prevention and routine repair: AirFlow (guided biofilm therapy), oral prophylaxis, and tooth-colored composite fillings. Start here if it has been more than six months since your last cleaning, or if a tooth has a visible hole but is not painful. Routine care is where most treatment plans begin.

**Cosmetic Dentistry.** Four treatments. From ₱12,000 [REPRESENTATIVE-PLACEHOLDER].

Direct composite veneers, porcelain veneers, zirconia veneers, and Zoom whitening. Material decides both the result and the fee, which is why the four are priced separately rather than bundled. Veneer cases are planned on a RayFace facial scan so the proportions are set against your face, not against a model on a bench.

**Orthodontics.** Two treatments. From ₱90,000 [REPRESENTATIVE-PLACEHOLDER].

Fixed braces and Invisalign (Clear Aligners), both fitted and monitored by in-house orthodontists. Treatment usually runs 12 to 30 months depending on how far the teeth have to travel. Clear aligner cases start with an iTero digital scan, so you see the projected movement before you commit.

**Oral Surgery.** Two treatments. From ₱6,000 [REPRESENTATIVE-PLACEHOLDER].

Surgical extractions and dental implants. Both are planned on 3D CBCT imaging first, because bone height, bone width and the position of the inferior alveolar nerve are decisions you want made from measured data. Piezotome ultrasonic instrumentation is used where bone must be cut while soft tissue is protected.

**Pediatric Dentistry.** Two treatments. From ₱1,200 [REPRESENTATIVE-PLACEHOLDER].

Fluoride treatment and dental sealants, the two interventions that do the most to keep a child out of the chair later. A sealant closes the deep grooves of a newly erupted molar before decay can start in them. Bring a child for a first visit before there is a problem, not after.

**Prosthodontics.** Four treatments. From ₱23,000 [REPRESENTATIVE-PLACEHOLDER].

Dental crowns, dental bridges, complete dentures and removable partial dentures. This is the field that rebuilds a bite rather than repairing one tooth, so the sequence and the order of appointments matter as much as the components. Finished work is checked on OccluSense digital bite analysis before it is cemented.

**Endodontics.** One treatment. From ₱15,000 [REPRESENTATIVE-PLACEHOLDER].

Root canal treatment, performed by endodontic specialists and planned on 3D CBCT imaging. A canal that does not appear on a flat radiograph is a common reason root canal treatment fails, and a three-dimensional scan is how that canal is found before the tooth is opened.

**Periodontics.** One treatment. From ₱8,000 [REPRESENTATIVE-PLACEHOLDER].

Scaling and root planing for gums that bleed, recede or have started to lose bone. Deep cleaning below the gum line is the treatment that decides whether teeth stay anchored, and it is the step most often postponed. Sol dental laser gum contouring is used where the tissue itself needs reshaping.

**Digital Dental X-rays.** One treatment. From ₱1,000 [REPRESENTATIVE-PLACEHOLDER].

Panoramic, cephalometric and periapical digital radiography, plus 3D CBCT imaging. Radiography is a treatment in its own right here because so many plans depend on it, and because you may want a scan and a second opinion before agreeing to work quoted somewhere else.

## §4. Not Sure What You Need

You do not have to name the field. Name what is wrong, or name what you want changed, and the field follows from that.

**A tooth aches, throbs at night, or hurts under pressure.** Start with endodontics. Decay that has reached the nerve is an endodontic problem, and a 3D CBCT scan shows how far it has gone before anything is opened.

**A tooth is sensitive to cold, or has a visible hole, but does not throb.** Start with general dentistry. Caught at this stage it is usually one appointment and one filling.

**Your gums bleed when you brush, or have started to pull back from the teeth.** Start with periodontics. Bleeding is not normal, and receding gums are a bone problem long before they are a cosmetic one.

**A tooth is missing, broken at the gum line, or has already been extracted.** Start with prosthodontics for a crown, bridge or denture, or with oral surgery if an implant is the right replacement. The consultation decides which, and the two fields plan it together.

**Your teeth are crowded, gapped or protruding.** Start with orthodontics. Both braces and clear aligners are available, and the choice depends on how far the teeth need to move rather than on preference alone.

**You want to change the shape, color or shade of your front teeth.** Start with cosmetic dentistry. Bring a photograph of what you are aiming at, because material and method are chosen from the target, not the other way round.

**Your child is due for a first visit, a sealant or a fluoride application.** Start with pediatric dentistry.

**You have been asked for a scan, or you want a second reading before treatment quoted elsewhere.** Start with digital dental X-rays. 3D CBCT imaging is available at all four clinics.

**You have been referred out of a practice before and would rather not repeat it.** Book the consultation and let the group route the case internally. That is what the bench is for.

If none of those describes your situation, the consultation is the right first step regardless. It is free, a specialist looks at the actual case, and it ends with a written quote before anything is scheduled.

## §5. The Technology Behind Every Treatment

Eight systems sit behind the twenty treatments above. They are named individually because named equipment is equipment you can ask about, check, and hold us to.

**3D CBCT imaging.** A three-dimensional view of teeth, jawbone, nerve canals and sinuses, used to plan implants, extractions and root canal treatment. Available at all four clinics.

**iTero intraoral scanner.** High-resolution digital impressions taken in minutes with a handheld wand, with no tray and no impression putty.

**RayFace facial scanner.** A 3D scan of your face, so veneers and smile design are planned against your own facial proportions rather than against an average.

**OccluSense bite analysis.** Digital measurement of where and how hard your teeth actually meet, used to check crowns, veneers and finished aligner cases.

**Sol Dental Laser.** Precise soft-tissue work and gum contouring, with less bleeding during the appointment.

**Piezotome.** Ultrasonic instrumentation that cuts bone while sparing the soft tissue around it, used in surgical extractions and implant site preparation.

**AirFlow (guided biofilm therapy).** Cleaning with air, warm water and a fine powder, reaching below the gum line and around brackets, implants and attachments.

**Zoom whitening.** Light-activated in-clinic whitening completed in a single session, with steps taken to limit sensitivity.

What each system does in detail, and what is installed where, is set out on the Technology page.
