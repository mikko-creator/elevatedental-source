## §2 - What this field covers

Cosmetic dentistry is the restorative and aesthetic layer of your care. It addresses how a tooth looks, how it sits next to the tooth beside it, and how the whole set reads against your lips and jawline.

The concerns treated in this field:

- Teeth that have darkened with age, coffee, tea or tobacco
- Chipped, worn or uneven edges
- Small gaps between the front teeth
- Teeth that are narrow, short or out of proportion with their neighbors
- Old composite work that has stained at the margins
- A gumline that sits high or low against the teeth
- Front teeth that are structurally sound but do not match

Cosmetic dentistry is one of the dental disciplines Elevate Dental holds in-house, alongside general dentistry, orthodontics, endodontics, periodontics, prosthodontics, oral surgery, pediatric dentistry and dental radiology. Cosmetic cases are where that matters most. A front tooth that looks wrong is often a bite problem, a gum problem or an alignment problem wearing a cosmetic disguise. When your assessment finds one, the orthodontist or the periodontist who handles it works in the same practice, on the same file, and the plan is revised once rather than restarted.

## §5 - Technology used here

Cosmetic work is measured before it is made. These systems are installed at Elevate Dental and used to plan and check it.

- **RayFace facial scanner.** We use a RayFace 3D facial scanner to plan veneers, crowns and smile design against the proportions of your actual face, rather than against the tooth in isolation.
- **iTero intraoral scanner.** A digital scan of your teeth in place of putty impression trays. The scan is the record your veneers are made to.
- **OccluSense bite analysis.** Digital pressure mapping that shows where your teeth actually meet, checked before new surfaces are fitted so the force lands where it should.
- **Zoom whitening.** Light-activated in-clinic whitening, used on its own or to set the shade the rest of a case is matched to.
- **3D CBCT imaging.** Available at all four clinics: Mitsukoshi BGC, Shangri-La Plaza, Bonifacio Stopover BGC and Alabang Town Center. Used when a cosmetic case sits on a tooth whose root or bone support needs assessing first.
