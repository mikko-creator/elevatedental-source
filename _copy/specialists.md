## §1. Hero and Filter

**H1**

Dental Specialists in Manila. Meet our 31 dentists.

**Deck**

Thirty-one published clinician profiles, across nine disciplines and four Metro Manila clinics. This is the roster, not a selection from it. Filter it down to the field your case sits in, read the profile of the person who would treat you, and book that appointment specifically. You are choosing a clinician here, not a building.

**Filter toolbar**

Filter by discipline: General Dentistry, Cosmetic Dentistry, Orthodontics, Oral Surgery, Pediatric Dentistry, Prosthodontics, Endodontics, Periodontics, Digital Dental X-rays.

Filter by treatment: the treatment you are considering, which narrows the roster to the clinicians who perform it.

Filter by clinic: Mitsukoshi BGC, Shangri-La Plaza, Bonifacio Stopover BGC, Alabang Town Center.

Sort by: Name, A to Z. Or by discipline, which groups the roster into its nine fields in the order shown above.

**Note under the toolbar**

Filters combine. Choosing a field and a clinic together shows only the clinicians who match both, and the result count updates as you go, so you always know how many people you are looking at.

## §2. Featured

**Heading**

A place to start, if you do not know which field you need.

**Body**

A short pinned set sits above the full list. It is not a ranking and it is not the whole roster. It is a small, deliberately chosen group, kept current, so that a first-time visitor who does not yet know whether their case is prosthodontic or periodontic still lands on a real clinician rather than an empty filter box.

Each pinned card carries the same information as every other card on this page: a photograph, the clinician's name, their credentials exactly as published, the field they practice in, and a link to the full profile. Nothing in the pinned set is presented that is not also true of the person in the list below.

If none of them matches your case, scroll. The full roster is directly underneath, and the filters are faster than reading.

**[OWNER-DATA] Featured selection.** Four pinned slots are reserved and the surrounding copy above is final, but the selection itself is an owner decision and has not been supplied. Do not auto-fill it from roster order, alphabetical order or recency, and do not select it by inference from the profile pages. Each slot needs a name, credentials as published, a field, and one sentence approved by that clinician.

## §3. The List

**Heading**

The full roster.

**Body**

Every published profile appears below. The list holds 31 specialists, and the count on this page and the count on the profile set are the same number by design: this page shows the bench, all of it, without a shortlist standing in front of it.

Each card carries a photograph, the clinician's name, their credentials as published, the field they practice in, and a link to the full profile. The profile is where the detail sits: training, the treatments that clinician performs, and the discipline hub their work belongs to. Read it before you book. A specialist page that tells you nothing beyond a name is not doing its job, and neither is a roster you cannot narrow.

The card is one template, stamped once per clinician, with the filter values carried on the card itself. That is what makes the toolbar above filter real content rather than decorate the page.

**[OWNER-DATA] Roster count.** The figure 31 was counted from the published profile set on 2026-08-13. It is load-bearing: it appears in this page's title, in this hero, in the homepage counters and in the treatments hub. Re-count it immediately before launch, and if it has moved, change every instance in the same pass. A roster number that is wrong in one place is worse than no roster number at all.

**[OWNER-DATA] Clinic assignment.** Which clinician practices at which clinic has not been confirmed by the owner. No clinician is assigned to a named clinic anywhere in this copy, and none may be assigned by inference. Until the per-clinic schedule is supplied, the clinic filter ships disabled with the label "Clinic filter available once schedules are confirmed", and the profile cards omit the clinic row rather than guessing at it.

**[OWNER-DATA] Portraits and credentials.** Clinician portraits exist in the asset set and are the intended imagery for these cards. Confirm before launch that each portrait matches its profile, that every credential string is current, and that the clinician has agreed to the photograph being published.

## §4. Routing

**Heading**

If the filter comes back empty, go sideways.

**Body**

A filter that returns nothing must say so in words. The empty state on this page reads: "No clinician matches that combination. Clear a filter, or choose a field below." It never renders as a blank panel, and it never leaves you at a dead end with no way out except the back button.

Three routes sit beneath the list, in this order.

**By field.** General Dentistry. Cosmetic Dentistry. Orthodontics. Oral Surgery. Pediatric Dentistry. Prosthodontics. Endodontics. Periodontics. Digital Dental X-rays. Each hub explains the field, lists the treatments inside it, and shows what those treatments start at.

**By treatment.** If you already know the treatment, go straight to it. Every treatment page names the field it belongs to and routes back here filtered to the right clinicians.

**By case, if you would rather not decide.** Book the consultation and let the group route it. Complex work is not meant to be sorted by the patient, and a case that turns out to span two fields is handled by two of our own specialists rather than sent out to a third party.

## §5. Closing CTA

**Heading**

You do not have to pick the right specialist yourself.

**Body**

If you know whose name you want, book that clinician directly from their profile. If you do not, book the consultation and the case is routed internally to the field it belongs to. Either way the first consultation is free, a specialist looks at the actual case, and you leave with a written quote before anything is scheduled.

**Primary action**

Book an Appointment
