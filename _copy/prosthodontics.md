## §2. What this field covers

A prosthodontist rebuilds what is missing or broken, and builds it so your bite can carry it for years.

The field covers four situations.

- **A tooth too damaged to hold a filling.** It is covered with a crown.
- **A gap where a tooth used to be.** It is closed with a fixed bridge or, where the case allows, an implant restoration.
- **Several missing teeth in the same arch.** They are replaced with a removable partial denture.
- **A jaw with no teeth left.** It is restored with a complete denture.

The field also covers the bite itself. Worn edges, a collapsed bite height and existing dentures that rock or rub are prosthodontic problems rather than cosmetic ones, and they are assessed as such.

Prosthodontics is one of the nine fields of dentistry practiced in-house at Elevate Dental. That is why a case needing an extraction first, or a root canal first, does not move to a different clinic between steps. The plan is written once and carried out by the people who wrote it.

## §5. Technology used here

Prosthodontic planning runs on named systems, not on unspecified equipment.

**3D CBCT imaging.** A three-dimensional scan of the jaw showing bone volume, root position and the nerve canal before a crown, bridge or implant restoration is planned. Available at all four clinics.

**iTero intraoral scanner.** A digital impression taken with a handheld scanner instead of a tray of impression material. The scan travels to the laboratory as a file.

**OccluSense bite analysis.** A digital record of where your teeth meet and how hard. The restoration is adjusted against that reading rather than against an estimate.

**RayFace facial scanner.** A 3D scan of your face, used to plan crowns and smile design against your actual facial proportions rather than a generic template.
