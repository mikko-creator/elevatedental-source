## §2 - What this field covers

General dentistry is the diagnostic and preventive layer of your care. Your dentist examines your teeth, gums and bite. Images are taken where an examination alone cannot answer the question. Plaque and tartar are removed. Teeth that have started to break down are restored before the damage reaches the nerve.

The conditions treated in this field:

- Tooth decay and cavities
- Plaque and tartar buildup above and below the gumline
- Early gum inflammation and bleeding
- Chipped, worn or cracked teeth
- Sensitivity to hot, cold and sweet
- Surface staining from coffee, tea and tobacco
- Routine monitoring of teeth that have already been filled, crowned or root-treated

General dentistry is one of the dental disciplines Elevate Dental holds in-house, alongside cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, endodontics, periodontics, prosthodontics, oral surgery, pediatric dentistry and dental radiology. That matters at one specific moment: when a routine check-up finds something outside a general dentist's scope. Your case moves to the right specialist inside the same practice, with your records, images and history already attached to it. You do not start again somewhere else.

## §5 - Technology used here

Diagnosis in general dentistry is only as good as what your dentist can see.

- **3D CBCT imaging.** Available at all four clinics: Mitsukoshi BGC, Shangri-La Plaza, Bonifacio Stopover BGC and Alabang Town Center. Used when a two-dimensional image cannot show the depth of a problem, such as a root fracture or a lesion under an existing restoration.
- **Digital radiography.** Low-dose digital images, viewable on screen in the same appointment rather than developed later.
- **AirFlow (guided biofilm therapy).** Removes biofilm and stain with air, water and fine powder, so cleaning reaches areas hand instruments pass over.
- **iTero intraoral scanner.** A digital scan of your teeth in place of putty impression trays, used for records and for planning restorative work.
