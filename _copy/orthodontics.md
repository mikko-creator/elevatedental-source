## §2 - What this field covers

Orthodontics is the alignment layer of your care. Your orthodontist takes records, reads how your upper and lower teeth meet, and sets a sequence of movements that brings them into a stable relationship.

The conditions treated in this field:

- Crowded or overlapping teeth
- Gaps and spacing between teeth
- Protruding upper front teeth
- Overbite, underbite and crossbite
- An open bite, where the front teeth do not meet when the back teeth do
- Teeth that have drifted after an extraction or a long-standing gap
- Relapse in adults who were treated years ago and stopped wearing retainers

Orthodontics is one of the dental disciplines Elevate Dental holds in-house, alongside general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, endodontics, periodontics, prosthodontics, oral surgery, pediatric dentistry and dental radiology. Alignment cases frequently need one of the others. Crowding can hide decay that has to be treated before brackets go on. A crossbite can involve a tooth that needs extracting first. Adult cases often finish with restorative work once the teeth are in place. Each of those handovers happens inside the same practice, on the same records, without your treatment plan going on hold while you find someone else.

## §3 - Treatments in this field

**Braces**
Fixed brackets and wires, adjusted at intervals through the whole course of treatment. Braces handle the widest range of cases, including the severe crowding and bite corrections aligners cannot address on their own. Fitted and monitored by in-house orthodontists. Starting price from ₱90,000 for full treatment [REPRESENTATIVE-PLACEHOLDER].
Link: Braces

**Invisalign (Clear Aligners)**
A sequence of removable clear aligners, each worn for a set period and changed on schedule, planned from a digital scan of your teeth. Discreet in normal conversation and removable for meals and cleaning, which also means the result depends on you wearing them for the hours your orthodontist specifies. Starting price from ₱180,000 [REPRESENTATIVE-PLACEHOLDER].
Link: Invisalign

**On timelines:** both routes are measured in months. Your orthodontist confirms the length of your case after records and a scan, because it is set by how far your teeth have to travel.

## §5 - Technology used here

Orthodontic planning is measurement first. These systems are installed at Elevate Dental and used to take and check those measurements.

- **iTero intraoral scanner.** A digital scan of your teeth in place of putty impression trays. The scan is what your aligner sequence is generated from, and it is repeated through treatment to compare progress against the plan.
- **Digital radiography.** Panoramic and cephalometric images that show the roots, the jaw relationship and the teeth still to erupt, viewable on screen in the same appointment.
- **3D CBCT imaging.** Available at all four clinics: Mitsukoshi BGC, Shangri-La Plaza, Bonifacio Stopover BGC and Alabang Town Center. Used where root position, an impacted tooth or the volume of bone around a tooth needs to be seen in three dimensions before teeth are moved.
