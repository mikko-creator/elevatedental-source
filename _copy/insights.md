## §1 - Hero and filter

**Eyebrow:** Insights and care

**H1:** Insights and Care

**Deck:** Plain answers on what treatments cost, how to choose between options, and how to look after your teeth between visits.

**Body:** Most people research a dental treatment for weeks before they book one. The two questions that decide it are almost always the same: what does this cost, and how do I choose between the options in front of me. This library is weighted toward those two, and written by the specialists who do the work.

Nothing here is a substitute for an examination. An article can tell you what a treatment involves and what it typically costs. It cannot tell you whether it is right for your case, because that requires looking at your teeth.

- **Strategic purpose, stated for the build not for the visitor.** This hub exists to contest the cost-intent queries currently answered by third-party directories rather than by a clinic. That intent shapes the pinned set and the standfirsts. It is never stated on the page, and no directory or competitor is named anywhere in this copy.
- **Rationed claims.** No specialist count, no discipline count, no one-roof phrasing, no testimonial count. All are rationed away from `insights`.
- **Currency.** No peso figure ships on this page. The migration note fixes the placeholder rule for every post that carries across.
- **Claim discipline for migrated posts.** The 185-post library was written before the claim rules existed and will contain retired claims. Migration is a rewrite pass, not a copy pass. This is called out in §3 rather than left to be discovered after launch.
