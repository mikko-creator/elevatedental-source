## §1 - Hero

**H1:** Pediatric Dentistry Philippines - Kids Dental Care

**Intro paragraph:**

Pediatric dentistry looks after the teeth your child has now and protects the ones still to come. It is mostly preventive: check-ups at a steady interval, sealants on the molars where decay starts, fluoride to strengthen enamel, and early sight of anything that will need attention later. Bring your child in when the first teeth appear rather than when something hurts. A first visit that involves nothing more than counting teeth is the one that makes every later visit easier.

## §2 - What this field covers

Pediatric dentistry is the preventive layer of your child's care, delivered at a pace a child can follow. Each step is explained before it is done, and the appointment is unhurried by design.

What is treated and monitored in this field:

- Decay in baby teeth, which spreads faster than in adult teeth
- Deep grooves in newly erupted molars, sealed before decay reaches them
- Enamel that needs strengthening in a child at higher risk of cavities
- Baby teeth that are late to fall out or adult teeth that are late to arrive
- Crowding visible in the mixed dentition, when both baby and adult teeth are present
- Thumb sucking and other habits that affect how the jaws develop
- Chipped or knocked teeth after a fall
- Brushing technique, diet and the habits that decide the next ten years

Pediatric dentistry is one of the dental disciplines Elevate Dental holds in-house, alongside general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, endodontics, periodontics, prosthodontics, oral surgery and dental radiology. For a child, that has a practical meaning. When a check-up shows crowding that will need orthodontics, or a tooth that has to come out to let another through, the specialist who handles it is in the same practice. Your child is not sent to an unfamiliar clinic to start the conversation over, and you are not managing two sets of appointments in two places.

A child's mouth changes for a decade, and the changes cross disciplines. Crowding becomes an orthodontic case. A tooth lost early becomes a spacing problem. A knocked front tooth becomes a restorative one. In a single-dentist practice each of those is a referral to somewhere new. ## §5 - Technology used here

With children the aim is to see what is needed and no more. These systems are installed at Elevate Dental and used with that in mind.

- **Digital radiography.** Low-dose digital images, taken only where an examination cannot answer the question, and viewable on screen in the same appointment so you can see what your dentist is describing.
- **3D CBCT imaging.** Available at all four clinics: Mitsukoshi BGC, Shangri-La Plaza, Bonifacio Stopover BGC and Alabang Town Center. Reserved for cases where a flat image is not enough, such as an unerupted or impacted adult tooth.
- **iTero intraoral scanner.** A digital scan in place of putty impression trays, which is easier for a child to sit through and is the record an early orthodontic assessment is built from.
- **AirFlow (guided biofilm therapy).** Cleaning with warm water, air and a fine powder rather than hand scraping, used where your dentist judges it suitable for your child.
