## §1 - Hero

**Eyebrow:** Contact

**H1:** Contact Elevate Dental

**Deck:** Four clinics, four direct lines, one appointment form. Call the clinic you want and you reach that clinic.

**Body:** There is no switchboard. Each clinic publishes its own number, and it is answered by the Front Desk Officer at that clinic. If you would rather write, the form below goes to the clinic you select on it.

## §2 - Appointment request form

**H2:** Request an appointment

**Body:** Seven fields. Enough for the clinic to prepare for you, and no more than that.

**Fields:**

- **Full name**
- **Contact number**
- **Email address**
- **Preferred clinic.** Mitsukoshi BGC, Shangri-La Plaza, Bonifacio Stopover BGC, Alabang Town Center.
- **Treatment you are interested in.** If you are not sure, leave it and say so in the message. Working out which treatment fits is what the consultation is for.
- **Preferred date**
- **Message.** Anything the clinic should know in advance: a medication, a previous treatment, an anxiety about the chair.

**Submit label:** Book an Appointment

**Under the form:** Your details are used to arrange this appointment. See our Privacy Policy for how records are held.

**If you would rather call:** The direct line for every clinic is in the next section.

## §4 - What happens next

**H2:** What happens next

**Body:** The clinic you selected picks up the request, confirms a time with you directly, and tells you what to bring. On the day, arrive 15 minutes early to complete any necessary paperwork, or complete the forms in advance from the New Patient Guide.

The first appointment is a comprehensive exam, digital X-rays if they are needed, and a discussion of your options. You leave knowing what is happening, what can be done, and in what order.

One thing to know before you book: Elevate Dental does not accept HMO or dental insurance. All treatment is self-pay.
