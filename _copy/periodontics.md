## §1. Hero

**Eyebrow:** Periodontics

**H1:** Periodontics Philippines: Gum Disease Treatment

**Standfirst:**

Periodontics treats the gum and bone that hold your teeth in place. Gum disease is quiet at the start: a little blood when you brush, and nothing that hurts. It does not clear up on its own. Treated at the bleeding stage it is controlled with cleaning below the gumline. Left alone it takes bone, and the bone does not come back by itself.

---

## §2. What this field covers

Your teeth are held by fibers anchored into bone. Bacteria collect at the gum margin and harden into calculus, and the body's response to that deposit breaks down the attachment. The gum separates from the tooth and a pocket forms. Once a pocket is deep enough, a toothbrush cannot reach the bottom of it, and no amount of brushing at home will.

A periodontist treats:

- gums that bleed when you brush or floss
- gums that have receded and left the root surface exposed
- pockets between gum and tooth that a routine cleaning cannot reach
- persistent bad breath coming from bacteria below the gumline
- teeth that have started to loosen or drift
- an uneven or heavy gum line, where contouring is indicated
- the gum and bone around an existing implant

Periodontics is one of the nine fields of dentistry practiced in-house at Elevate Dental, and it sits underneath most other dental work. Crowns, implants and orthodontic treatment all depend on stable bone. Having the periodontist in the same clinic means the foundation is assessed before anything is planned on top of it, rather than after a restoration has already been made.

## §4. The specialists in this field

Gum disease is diagnosed by measurement, not by appearance. A periodontist charts the depth of every pocket around every tooth, reads bone level against the imaging, and decides from those numbers what the tissue can recover and what it cannot. Gums can look healthy over a pocket that is losing bone.

That is the case for keeping this in-house. A general practice that finds deep pockets sends the case out to somebody else, and the treatment restarts with a clinician who has not seen the chart. Here the charting, the treatment and the maintenance schedule stay with one team.

## §5. Technology used here

Periodontal treatment is measurement first, then instrumentation.

**3D CBCT imaging.** Shows bone level around each root in three dimensions, so the extent of the loss is measured rather than estimated from a flat film. Available at all four clinics.

**Sol Dental Laser.** Used for gum contouring where it is indicated, and for soft-tissue work that would otherwise call for a scalpel and sutures.

**AirFlow (guided biofilm therapy).** Lifts biofilm and surface staining with a controlled jet of air, water and fine powder. Used at maintenance appointments after periodontal treatment, while the tissue is still settling and instrumentation would be heavy-handed.
